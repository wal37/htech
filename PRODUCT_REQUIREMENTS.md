# Healthcare Connect Product Requirements

## Product Summary
Healthcare Connect is a government-ready digital health platform that connects patient records, care coordination, public-health reporting, analytics, AI-assisted decision support, and cloud operations in one secure system.

## Objectives
- Improve continuity of care across facilities and programs
- Reduce fragmentation in health data and operational workflows
- Increase reporting accuracy and speed for health authorities
- Provide secure, auditable, role-based access to regulated data
- Support AI-assisted planning and decision support within governed workflows

## Core User Groups
- Ministries of health
- Regional health authorities
- Public hospital systems
- National insurance and payer teams
- Clinicians and care coordinators
- Health information officers
- Public health analysts
- Program administrators

## Functional Requirements

### 1. Patient Identity and Record Management
- Create and manage unified patient profiles
- Store encounter history, medications, allergies, diagnoses, and documents
- Support record updates across authorized facilities
- Maintain longitudinal care history

### 2. Referral and Coordination Workflows
- Create digital referrals
- Route patients between facilities or specialists
- Track follow-up, discharge, and escalation workflows
- Support appointment and service-status visibility

### 3. Facility and Provider Network Management
- Maintain provider and facility directories
- Show service availability and network relationships
- Support coordination across hospital, lab, pharmacy, and program sites

### 4. Public Health Reporting
- Generate facility, district, state, and national reports
- Track immunization, maternal health, chronic care, and disease program outputs
- Support surveillance and outbreak-related reporting

### 5. Claims and Program Administration
- Support public payer, claims, and approval workflows
- Track utilization and reimbursement context
- Flag unusual or suspicious patterns for review

### 6. Analytics and Dashboards
- Facility performance dashboards
- Population and program metrics
- Resource demand and service bottleneck visibility
- Regional comparison and executive reporting

### 7. AI-Assisted Decision Support
- Clinical summarization
- Risk and triage support
- Demand forecasting
- Anomaly detection
- Population-risk analysis
- Explainable assistive outputs with oversight

### 8. Citizen and Patient Access
- Appointment and referral status
- Reminders and notifications
- Secure access to records or summaries where allowed

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Role-based access control
- Audit trails for all critical actions
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Backup and disaster recovery
- High availability for critical workflows
- API-first interoperability
- Configurable deployment by region or institution
- Support for sovereign or approved cloud environments

## Security and Compliance Requirements
- Data retention controls
- Access logging
- Incident monitoring
- Consent and policy-aware access where required
- Secure integration with external systems
- Administrative review and governance tooling

## Integration Requirements
- Hospital information systems
- Laboratory information systems
- Pharmacy systems
- Claims and insurance systems
- Reporting systems
- Identity and directory systems where allowed
- External analytics or data warehouses

## Deployment Phases

### Phase 1
- Patient identity
- Facility network
- Referral workflows
- Operational dashboard baseline

### Phase 2
- Public health reporting
- Claims and program administration
- Expanded interoperability

### Phase 3
- AI decision support
- Forecasting
- Anomaly detection
- Broader regional or national scale rollout

## Success Metrics
- Referral turnaround time
- Reduction in fragmented record handling
- Reporting completeness and timeliness
- Facility or program visibility improvements
- Time saved in case handling and operational review
- Adoption across institutions and user groups
