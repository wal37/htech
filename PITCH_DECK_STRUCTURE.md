# Healthcare Connect Pitch Deck Structure

## Positioning
Healthcare Connect should be presented as secure public-sector digital health infrastructure, not a wellness app, generic clinic SaaS, or standalone EMR.

## Slide Structure

1. `Cover`
Healthcare Connect
Government-ready digital health infrastructure for care coordination, data, AI, and cloud operations.

2. `Problem`
- Fragmented patient records and weak continuity across facilities
- Slow referrals and care routing
- Poor visibility for ministries and health authorities
- Disconnected reporting and under-governed data systems

3. `Solution`
- Unified platform for records, referrals, care coordination, reporting, analytics, and AI-assisted operations

4. `Why now`
- Public-sector digitization pressure
- Higher demand for health-system visibility
- AI adoption requiring governed deployment

5. `Platform overview`
- Inputs
- Core platform
- Outputs

6. `Core modules`
- Unified patient record
- Referral and coordination
- Facility and provider network
- Claims and program administration
- Public health reporting
- Citizen access

7. `Government use cases`
- National programs
- Regional oversight
- Regulated provider networks

8. `Workflow`
- Register
- Coordinate
- Report
- Improve

9. `Trust and governance`
- Role-based access
- Audit trails
- Data protection
- Public-sector oversight

10. `Target market`
- Ministries and agencies
- Public hospitals
- Insurance and program operators

11. `Differentiation`
- Government-ready
- Clinical + public-health + administrative workflows
- Governed AI
- Interoperable cloud foundation

12. `Deployment model`
- Phase 1: core coordination
- Phase 2: reporting and claims
- Phase 3: AI and national interoperability

13. `Roadmap`
- Core operations
- Reporting expansion
- AI support
- National scale integration

14. `Business model`
- Government platform contracts
- Enterprise health deployments
- Integration services
- Premium AI and analytics modules

15. `Close`
Healthcare Connect modernizes public and regulated healthcare systems by connecting care, oversight, and intelligence in one platform.
