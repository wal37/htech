Privacy Policy

How Healthcare Connect handles platform, partner, and inquiry-related information.

This policy explains how Healthcare Connect may collect, use, and protect information for website inquiries, platform evaluation, and future product operations.

Scope

Website inquiries, product evaluation, partner communication, and future platform operations.

Control model

Administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards aligned with regulated environments.

Contact point

enquiry@healthcareconnect.ca

01

Information we collect

We may collect contact details, organization details, inquiry content, uploaded materials, product evaluation information, and limited operational data needed to support communication, security, and service planning.

02

How we use it

We use information to respond to inquiries, support product access, improve service quality, maintain platform security, and help healthcare and government stakeholders evaluate Healthcare Connect.

03

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Limited information may be shared with authorized infrastructure, hosting, or service partners where necessary to operate the platform or support contracted services.

04

Retention

Information may be retained for customer relationship management, compliance, platform operations, security review, and regulated service support as required by law or contract.

05

Security controls

We apply administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to platform risk, including access control, encryption, backup, monitoring, and audit support.

06

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact enquiry@healthcareconnect.ca.
